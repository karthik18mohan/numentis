The gap

Plenty of openings. Few ready hands.

Companies prefer people who can do the work from the first day. Most graduates have the degree, but have never posted a purchase bill, prepared a GST return or closed a month.

Skills Lab closes that gap by doing the work. You learn each task the way an accounts office runs it, on real documents and in the software offices use, with practitioners beside you.

Brand position

Numentis is the finance services group for businesses that take finance seriously — and the next generation of professionals who will run them.
