Privacy notice

Your data, used onlyas you agree.

Who we are

This notice covers Numentis Group and its businesses: Numentis Audit & Advisory, Numentis Next, Numentis Skills Lab and Numentis Strategy & Finance ("Numentis", "we").

What we collect

What you give us in a form, chat, call or message: your name, mobile number, the course you are asking about, your stage of study or work, your preferred language and your message. We also record which page and advertisement brought you to us, and the date and time of the consent you gave.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, arrange counselling, process an admission and send the messages you agreed to. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for any other purpose without asking you.

AI assistants

Some replies on WhatsApp and some calls started from this website are handled by an AI assistant, which says so at the start. You can ask for a human counsellor at any time.

Who else handles it

Service providers who run our website, lead records, WhatsApp, AI assistant and telephone systems handle your data for us, only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an admission are deleted after 24 months. Student records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent at any time, raise a grievance, and nominate someone to act for you. Withdrawing consent stops future contact; it does not undo contact already made.

Cookies

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies.

Contact

A privacy contact will be published here soon. Until then, ask a counsellor and they will pass your request on.
