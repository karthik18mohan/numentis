Basic Level

The six modules.

Module

01

Foundation and order-to-cash

Accounting as it is practised in an office: documents, entries and controls. Then the sales side: quotations, invoices, receivables, customer ledgers and collections.

Module

02

Procure-to-pay and inventory

Purchase orders, goods receipt, vendor bills, payables and payments, with stock records kept in step.

Module

03

Record-to-report and banking

From journals and ledgers to the trial balance and financial statements. Bank reconciliations, and the paperwork behind letters of credit and bank guarantees.

Module

04

Statutory compliance: TDS, GST, payroll

GST and TDS returns prepared under faculty supervision. Payroll with PF and ESI workings.

Module

05

Advanced tools: TallyPrime, ERP, Power BI

The same work done in the software offices use: TallyPrime, Zoho Books and ERP workflows, Excel for checks, and Power BI for reports.

Module

06

Capstone, interview preparation and graduation

A capstone that brings the five modules together, preparation for interviews, and graduation with the completion certificate.

Questions

Before you ask.

Who is the programme for?

Final-year commerce students, fresh B.Com, M.Com, BBA and MBA graduates, and junior accountants in their first two years at work. A counselling session confirms whether it fits you.

How long is it, and where are classes held?

Four months, in six modules. Classes are in person, in our classroom in Calicut (Kozhikode).

What does it cost?

The fee is ₹50,000. If you pay the full fee at once, it is ₹40,000. Instalment plans are explained at counselling.

When does the next batch start?

Batch dates will be announced soon. Book a counselling session and we will tell you as soon as they are fixed.
